How to play
Lower the bridge. Turn up the falls. Wait for dark.

Tap a tower to send up a firework · tap the gate to lower or raise the drawbridge · tap the ship for a royal salute · tap the brazier to stoke it · drag across the lake to make waves · drag the side of the block to tip it · drag a pine to bend it · throw wishing coins into the pool under the falls · slide the time of day to nightfall.
